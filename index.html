Client Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about commissioning your bespoke garment.

What is your typical turnaround time for bespoke tailoring? ▼
Our standard bespoke turnaround is 7 to 10 working days from measurement confirmation. For urgent wedding dates or executive banquets, we provide a 72-Hour VIP Rush Express option.
Can I order from outside Nigeria (UK, USA, Canada)? ▼
Yes! Over 40% of our bespoke commissions are shipped to diaspora clients. We conduct a thorough virtual measurement consultation via WhatsApp video to guide you step-by-step, followed by door-to-door express courier via DHL Express (typically 3–5 business days transit).
Do you provide fabric, or can I bring my own material? ▼
Both options are welcomed. We maintain an in-atelier archive of original Austrian and Swiss cotton voiles, English cashmeres, and silks. Alternatively, you may bring your own preferred fabric for precision cutting and embroidery.
How do you handle wedding grooms and groomsmen packages? ▼
We coordinate full wedding entourages ranging from 3 to 20+ groomsmen. We coordinate synchronized fabric dyeing, matching custom linings, personalized monograms, and dedicated group fitting milestones to ensure zero stress on the wedding day.
